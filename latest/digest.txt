📚 Daily Knowledge — August 21, 2026

🌐 Top World News
• A Shenzhen court sentenced Evergrande founder Hui Ka-yan to life in prison for large-scale financial fraud and bribery, confiscating his personal property and fining Evergrande and its Hengda unit a combined roughly $2.5 billion, closing a chapter on the property giant's 2020 collapse with over $300 billion in liabilities.
• Israel's military announced its first criminal investigations into troop conduct in Gaza, focusing on the killings of 5-year-old Hind Rajab and her family and of 15 Palestinian paramedics, while separately striking an air base in northwest Syria to try to prevent Turkish troops from deploying there.
• Iraq's prime minister, who promised President Trump in a July Washington visit that Iraq would disarm Iran-backed militias by the end of September, faces that deadline amid ongoing regional tension as the U.S. Treasury also vows fresh "toughest ever" sanctions pressure on Iran.

💻 Technology
• Google is deepening its bet on custom AI silicon through a major agreement with Marvell Technology that could give Google a stake worth as much as $12.2 billion in the chipmaker.
• OpenAI reportedly paused a major training run after experimental models crossed internal safety boundaries, as researchers separately uncovered signs of near-autonomous AI agents being used in real-world cyberattacks.
• Amazon is preparing its largest-ever Prime Air drone-delivery expansion, aiming to bring the service to nearly 500 U.S. cities and towns by the end of 2026 — roughly six times its current footprint — even as U.S. officials warn hackers are targeting industrial controllers tied to water, energy and manufacturing systems.

🔬 Science: Researchers reported that dozens of animal species carry enzymes capable of breaking down biodegradable plastic, while separately a promising cancer-vaccine trial suggested the approach could be extended to target other tumor types.

📜 History: On this day in 1959, Hawaii officially became the 50th U.S. state, capping a decades-long path to statehood for the Pacific island territory.

🤔 Philosophy: Philosophers are grappling with how rapidly accelerating technology and science's shift from explanation to intervention are straining traditional frameworks for knowledge, meaning and responsibility.

🌍 Geography: Scientists reported a newly discovered Arctic process in which sunlight reacting with chemicals released by ocean algae and melting sea ice can dramatically boost the particles that seed cloud formation.

💰 Economics: U.S. consumer prices rose 3.4% year-over-year in July — a slight easing from prior months — even as the economy unexpectedly shed 23,000 jobs and growth slowed to a 1.5% pace in the second quarter.

🧠 Psychology: A new study found that people who watched more television in midlife had smaller brain volumes decades later in regions tied to language and thinking, underscoring long-term links between screen habits and cognitive health.

🎨 Art & Literature: Self-taught Jersey artist Adam Perchard won the inaugural Hogarth Prize for his work "Leviathan," earning £3,000 and a solo exhibition at Hogarth's House in Chiswick.

🔭 Astronomy & Space: NASA has pulled forward the launch of its Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope to as early as August 30, 2026; once in orbit, its field of view — at least 100 times larger than Hubble's — will let astronomers survey the universe far more efficiently.

🧬 Biology & Evolution: Geneticists reported that modern humans carry DNA from two previously unknown extinct hominin lineages beyond Neanderthals and Denisovans, revealing a more tangled history of ancient interbreeding than once thought.

🗺️ World Cultures & Languages: Draft legislation before China's National People's Congress would enact a new language law formally establishing Mandarin's status, part of a broader global picture in which roughly 40% of the world's 7,000-plus languages are considered endangered.

➗ Mathematics: The 2026 Fields Medals were announced at the ICM in Philadelphia, with Yu Deng recognized for breakthroughs on Hilbert's Sixth Problem, alongside Clay Research Awards for work proving the Furstenberg set and Kakeya conjectures.

⚖️ Politics & Law: Maryland privacy advocates allege data brokers are violating one of the nation's strictest state data-privacy laws by selling personal data to police and federal immigration agencies.

🎵 Music: The 2026 MTV Video Music Award nominations were revealed on August 18, arriving the same week Lollapalooza's third day in Chicago was disrupted by severe overnight flooding at Grant Park.
