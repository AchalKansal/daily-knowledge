📚 Daily Knowledge — August 25, 2026

🌐 Top World News
• A Ukrainian drone strike on the Taneco oil refinery in Nizhnekamsk, deep inside Russia's Tatarstan region, killed at least 13 people — including citizens of Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan and a child — in the deadliest attack to hit Russia in more than two years, part of Kyiv's near-daily long-range campaign against Russian refineries.
• In Gaza, the US-backed Board of Peace has negotiated a disarmament agreement with Hamas, but the group says it is conditional on a full Israeli withdrawal, which Israel has not formally accepted, leaving the ceasefire framework in limbo.
• Evergrande founder Hui Ka Yan was sentenced to life in prison, a milestone in the collapse of the Chinese property giant, which went down owing more than $300 billion in liabilities and helped trigger China's ongoing real-estate crisis.

💻 Technology
• China's XPeng raised more than $900 million to fund humanoid robot development, while Alibaba paired a new AI video model with billions of dollars in fresh infrastructure spending, underscoring the scale of China's AI and robotics push.
• Perplexity is reportedly seeking a new funding round at a valuation far above its roughly $20 billion mark from last year, with annualized revenue said to have grown from under $250 million at the start of 2026 to more than $750 million.
• The AI infrastructure buildout continues to reshape markets: Amazon has raised device prices as component costs climb, and power-infrastructure companies are increasingly becoming acquisition targets amid surging data-center demand.

🔬 Science: A new AI model has produced the first detailed estimate of the world's glacier volume outside Greenland and Antarctica, putting it at roughly 150,000 cubic kilometers of ice — equivalent to about 32 cm of potential global sea-level rise.

📜 History: On August 25, 1944, Paris was liberated by Allied and Free French forces after four years of Nazi occupation during World War II.

🤔 Philosophy: Philosophers continue to mourn Anthony Kenny, the Oxford professor emeritus and leading figure in philosophy of mind, while current debate in outlets like The Philosophers' Magazine centers on Kit Fine and Timothy Williamson's dispute over "hyperintensionality."

🌍 Geography: Greenland's Petermann Glacier calved a 76.4-square-kilometer ice island on August 4 — roughly the size of Manhattan and the glacier's largest loss of floating ice since 2012 — giving scientists a rare chance to study how massive Arctic ice masses break apart.

💰 Economics: The Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge came in at 2.2% in August, cooler than expected, but futures markets now price only about a 33% chance of a September rate cut as officials, including incoming Chair Kevin Warsh, weigh persistent price pressures against slowing growth.

🧠 Psychology: New research finds people sleeping roughly 6.4 to 7.8 hours a night show the lowest markers of biological aging, adding to evidence that both too little and too much sleep can accelerate age-related decline.

🎨 Art & Literature: The 2026 Booker Prize longlist of 13 novels — the "Booker Dozen" — was announced, chosen from 163 submissions by a panel chaired by classicist Mary Beard and including musician-writer Jarvis Cocker.

🔭 Astronomy & Space: NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope has been cleared for final launch preparations ahead of a targeted August 30 liftoff on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy, with a field of view over 100 times larger than Hubble's.

🧬 Biology & Evolution: A 240-million-year-old reptile fossil discovered in Brazil is offering new clues about what evolution looked like just before dinosaurs and crocodile relatives rose to dominance.

🗺️ World Cultures & Languages: Nearly 40% of the world's roughly 7,000 languages are now considered endangered, with Oceania hardest hit and nearly all Indigenous languages in North and Central America at risk, driving new documentation efforts like the Endangered Languages Documentation Programme's ongoing grant work.

➗ Mathematics: Computer scientists announced the first improvement in 30 years to a classic combinatorics problem about evenly allocating objects between two groups, a "huge breakthrough" reported in the math of imbalance.

⚖️ Politics & Law: Congressional Democrats have proposed legislation to expand the U.S. Supreme Court from nine to 13 justices and cap terms at 18 years, alongside a binding ethics code and new limits on the court's "shadow docket."

🎵 Music: Nigerian star Ayra Starr released her album "Starr Girl" via Mavin Records, part of a packed late-August release slate that also includes a newly unearthed Prince collection, "Timeless," and fresh projects from K-pop acts ENHYPEN and NCT 127.
