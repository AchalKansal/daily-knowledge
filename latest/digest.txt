🔬 Science: Scientists have generated quantum entanglement directly from sunlight for the first time, a potential lower-energy alternative to the lasers normally required to produce entangled photons for quantum technology.

📜 History: On this day in 1945, three days after Hiroshima, a U.S. B-29 Superfortress code-named "Bockscar" dropped the atomic bomb "Fat Man" on Nagasaki, Japan, killing an estimated 74,000 people and hastening the end of World War II.

🤔 Philosophy: With AI ethics increasingly treated as core infrastructure rather than an afterthought, 2026 is emerging as a pivotal year for philosophers debating how — and whether — ethical guardrails can be built into AI systems from the ground up.

💰 Economics: The U.S. economy shed 23,000 jobs in July, a sharp miss against forecasts of an 80,000-job gain and the first monthly decline in years; unemployment still fell to 4.1%, but only because nearly 1.4 million people have left the labor force so far this year.

🧠 Psychology: A new study published August 3 in Scientific Reports shows that predicting upcoming words is a core, active part of how humans understand language — a mechanism that stays dynamically engaged from childhood through old age.

🎨 Art & Literature: The Booker Prize 2026 longlist of 13 novels, chosen from 163 submissions, was revealed this week, while the American Academy of Arts and Letters awarded its $100,000 Christopher Lightfoot Walker Award to novelist Joan Silber.
