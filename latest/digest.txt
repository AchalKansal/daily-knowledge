📚 Daily Knowledge — September 18, 2026

🌐 Top World News
• President Trump told Axios he faces a "big decision" on whether to "go in and annihilate" the Iranian regime, ahead of a planned Tuesday meeting with six Gulf leaders at the UN General Assembly that could determine whether the seven-month US-Iran war turns to renewed diplomacy or intensified strikes.
• A UN International Fact-Finding Mission said it found reasonable grounds to believe the United States was behind February strikes on a school and sports facility in Iran, concluding the attacks constituted war crimes.
• India pushed back on a US threat of 100% tariffs over its purchases of Russian oil, with New Delhi vowing to protect its energy security and economic interests as trade tensions with Washington escalate.

💻 Technology
• Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a new cybersecurity-focused AI model for government and enterprise customers that it says can detect and patch software vulnerabilities at frontier-level performance while running faster and cheaper than larger models.
• OpenAI said an internal model helped prove that the Navier-Stokes equations, which describe how fluids move, are "fatally flawed" — a striking claimed math breakthrough announced September 8 that has drawn skepticism from mathematicians.
• A supply-chain data breach at market-research firm Klue exposed information from roughly 200 companies, including cybersecurity names Jamf, HackerOne and LastPass, while CISA separately warned that Iranian hackers targeted more than 100 US water utilities over the summer.

🔬 Science: Scientists say the 2022 Hunga Tonga volcanic eruption may have triggered an unexpected chemical reaction that destroyed some of the methane it released into the atmosphere, a finding that could reshape how researchers estimate and combat methane pollution.

📜 History: On September 18, 1851, the first edition of The New York Times was published in New York City, founded by Henry Jarvis Raymond and George Jones.

🤔 Philosophy: A new complete translation of Plato's Dialogues has been published in print and made freely available online by the Foundation for Platonic Studies, opening the classical texts to a wider modern audience.

🌍 Geography: Marine scientists rediscovered a deep coral reef off the coast of Benin thought to have died out more than 60 years ago, finding healthy coral patches and multiple fish species thriving over 50 meters below the surface.

💰 Economics: Markets are bracing for further Fed tightening after a hotter-than-expected August CPI report, with oil prices up sharply this month on Middle East tensions and the Bank of England instead holding rates steady even as UK inflation climbed to 3.1%.

🧠 Psychology: The largest-ever genetic study of borderline personality disorder identified 11 genome regions linked to the condition, revealing shared genetic risk with PTSD, depression and ADHD.

🎨 Art & Literature: The Booker Prize will announce its 2026 shortlist of six novels on September 22 at London's Southbank Centre, with a judging panel chaired by classicist Mary Beard alongside poet Raymond Antrobus and musician Jarvis Cocker.

🔭 Astronomy & Space: NASA's Roman Space Telescope launched successfully on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy, beginning a three-month, million-mile journey to its final orbit on its $4.3 billion mission to survey the universe.

🧬 Biology & Evolution: Paleontologists identified a giant new dinosaur species from Brazil that stretched roughly 20 meters long and roamed the region about 120 million years ago.

🗺️ World Cultures & Languages: Nearly 40% of the world's roughly 7,000 languages are now considered endangered, with community-led revitalization efforts — including apps supporting Australia's Yugambeh language — working to pass them on to younger generations.

➗ Mathematics: Mathematicians disproved a geometry rule that had stood for 150 years, discovering two differently shaped doughnut-like surfaces that appear identical under local measurement but are in fact globally distinct.

⚖️ Politics & Law: The Supreme Court issued a brief ruling in USPS v. California blocking an attempt to restrict mail-in voting ahead of the 2026 midterms, with only Justices Alito and Thomas dissenting.

🎵 Music: Luke Bryan released his ninth studio album, "Signs," today via MCA Nashville and Row Crop Records, while Anthrax also put out a new album, "Cursum Perficio."
